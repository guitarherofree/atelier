# Changelog

## 1.0.0
- Discover residencies, grants and open calls from ArtConnect, Res Artis, Art Infoland, Artist Communities Alliance, Rivet, TransArtists, Artenda and Piney Wood Atlas, plus your own sources, pasted links, entries by hand, web searches and Instagram #artistresidency (browse and capture, or the Graph API).
- Every detail shows where it came from, when it was last checked, and whether to verify it. Saved opportunities are re-checked and changes are flagged; your own edits are never overwritten.
- Fit explanations from your artist profile, goals and priorities, with an optional AI explanation.
- Applications with materials checklists, tailored proposal copies (your original never changes), answers with word and character limits, work samples, recommendation requests, and a review that you must confirm before anything goes out.
- Portfolio library, reusable proposals with a guided form and versions, and saved application text.
- Calendar with export to Apple Calendar, and a map that lists online opportunities separately.
- Optional AI (Ollama on your Mac, Claude with your API key, or an OpenAI-compatible server), off by default, with an activity log.
- Daily automatic backups, one-click restore, readable export, and in-app updates from GitHub releases.
